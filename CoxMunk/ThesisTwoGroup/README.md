# Two-group Elfouhaily and modified-Lie MSS validation

This is a clean validation implementation. It does not call the earlier
four-group Cox-Munk scripts.

## Groups

1. `Linear Elfouhaily`: a directional Elfouhaily realization generated by
   linear spectral filtering.
2. `Modified Lie Breaking`: the paired realization after the second-order,
   wind-direction-dependent modified Lie term in thesis equations
   (2.31)-(2.33).

In equation (2.31), `h_tx` and `h_ty` are implemented as the two Riesz
(two-dimensional Hilbert) components of elevation. They are not interpreted
as ordinary spatial derivatives; that interpretation creates an unphysical
high-wavenumber amplification at low wind speed.

Both groups use identical random coefficients. The only group difference is
the modified Lie term in the primary-wave band.

## Multiscale sampling

The primary grid uses `dk = kp/12`, satisfying the thesis requirement
`dk <= kp/10`. Independent octave tiles synthesize the unresolved short-wave
bands up to `pi*1000 rad/m`. Their directional slope variances are additive,
so this preserves an explicitly simulated optical-scale MSS without requiring
one enormous domain at millimetre spacing.

## References and outputs

The wind grid is fixed at `U10 = 1:1:10 m/s`, with 20 paired realizations per
wind speed. Results are compared with:

- Cox-Munk along-, cross-, and total-MSS laws;
- Guérin IASI values where available (`3-10 m/s` here);
- the Hu total-MSS law and anisotropy values `0.864` and `0.84` summarized by
  Gao et al., IEEE TGRS 2024;
- the numerical integral of the exact Elfouhaily spectrum used by the code.
